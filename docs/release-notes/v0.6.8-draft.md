> ## Why this one matters
> **v0.6.8 is about the client telling the truth about failures it does not
> cause.** The headline case is real and recent: a miner whose machine could not
> reach our servers at all was told, in our own words, that *"(address, device) is
> not on the allow-list, so this channel will not earn credit."* That sentence was
> wrong. It sent the miner looking at their account and sent us looking at a relay
> configuration — while the actual problem was an HTTPS connection that never left
> their network. **A client that guesses a cause is worse than a client that says
> it does not know**, and this release removes a whole family of those guesses:
> in the error text, in the AI/training loops that said "waiting for a placement"
> while holding no seat, and in the payout-address check that said "valid" about
> an address the server would refuse.
>
> ## 这一版为什么重要
> **v0.6.8 讲的是:客户端要对"不是自己造成的失败"说实话。** 起因是一个真实且新近的
> 案例:一台**根本连不到我方服务器**的矿机,被我们自己的文案告知*"(地址,设备)未进入
> 允许名单,因此此通道不会计入积分"*。这句话是错的。它把矿工引向了去查自己的账户,
> 也把我们引向了去查中继配置 —— 而真正的问题是一条**从未离开对方网络**的 HTTPS 连接。
> **一个瞎猜原因的客户端,比一个承认"我分不清"的客户端更糟。** 本版本清掉了一整类
> 这样的猜测:在错误文案里,在那些"明明没有座位却显示等待分配"的 AI/训练循环里,
> 以及在那个"对着服务端会拒绝的地址说 valid"的返还地址校验里。

## English

### 1. Error messages that no longer invent a cause

The old text rendered **any** transport-layer failure that mentioned our `/m4/`
route as an allow-list rejection. So a company firewall, an HTTPS-inspecting
router, or an antivirus TLS proxy — none of which our server ever sees — came out
as "your address is not enrolled". Three regions failing at once made it look
even more like an account problem, while the browser on the same machine opened
our website perfectly.

v0.6.8 first answers a narrower, answerable question: **how far did the request
actually get?**

| what we observed | what we now say |
|---|---|
| an HTTP status came back | the server answered — only `401`/`403` may mention the allow-list |
| TLS / certificate failure | the connection was intercepted or its certificate could not be verified; **not** an account problem |
| DNS / connect / timeout | we could not reach the server from this machine |
| a reply we cannot decode | the answer was unreadable — cause unknown |
| none of the above | **we cannot tell** — and it says so, in those words |

A TLS failure now comes with the three checks that actually resolve it: retry on
a phone hotspot, allow `us,asia,eu.aliceprotocol.org` through the security
software, or pin a region with `--region`. `429` is named as rate-limiting, `5xx`
as **our** fault, and any other `4xx` explicitly says it may not be the
allow-list.

The same correction was applied to the supervisor's relay messages. "All relays
unavailable" is a claim about **our** infrastructure that the client cannot
verify; it now reads **"no relay reachable from here"**. (This is exactly the
wording that turned a client-side watchdog bug into a false "region outage"
alarm on 2026-07-25.)

**A correction we owe the record:** the audit that prompted this work said the
client bundles its own root certificates and ignores the OS trust store. That
was true of older builds; **v0.6.7 already reads the system trust store** on all
platforms. So we still do **not** know why that miner's three regions failed
together — a locally-installed inspection CA and a plain TCP/DNS block are both
consistent with the evidence. v0.6.8's job is that the client no longer pretends
to know either.

### 2. AI and training loops that stop claiming to be online

- **Crash loops are now bounded.** A placement that keeps killing its engine used
  to have its failure budget reset by the next poll — the same broken assignment
  could restart forever. The budget now follows a stable fingerprint of the
  placement across polls, and a given-up placement gets a single probe after an
  escalating cooldown, not a fresh budget. Time spent genuinely serving refunds
  budget; a placement that never serves does not.
- **A lost seat is repaired, not narrated.** Losing the seat (or three failed
  heartbeats) now stops the engine and enters an explicit **re-registering**
  state with backoff. Previously the client kept showing "waiting for the center
  to place this stage" while it was no longer in the scheduling pool at all.
- **Readiness is measured on the right port, and says where it came from.** The
  probe used the port from your local config instead of the one the center
  assigned. Head nodes, which are not supposed to listen at all, could sit in
  `launching` forever — or be declared ready because an unrelated program held
  the port. The status line now distinguishes "the engine said it is listening"
  from "a port accepts connections (this is a probe, not the engine's own word)".
- **Unknown protocol answers are no longer filed as "waiting".** Every status
  except `no_assignment` — including a server error envelope — used to render as
  a patient, normal-looking wait. Now anything unrecognised is surfaced as a
  protocol error with a sanitised status and reason code.
- **Incomplete or expiring leases are refused before the GPU starts.** A training
  lease missing its entry point, its held-out commitment, or its expiry used to
  be accepted with empty strings — a full generation spent on a task that could
  not be verified or was already dead. All three are now required and
  format-checked, and the deadline is checked **before generating, before each
  retry, and before submitting**.
- **AI/training child processes join the same process-group / Job Object
  supervision as the mining engines**, so a killed run cannot leave descendants
  holding VRAM and bandwidth.

### 3. Single instance, honest about what it cannot know

Two miners sharing one data directory corrupt each other's bookkeeping. v0.6.8
takes an atomic lock: a second instance **refuses to start** and names the
running pid, the shared directory, and what to do about it — with
`--allow-multiple` as the documented escape hatch.

Two deliberate limits, stated rather than hidden: when the probe **cannot tell**
whether the other process is alive, the miner **starts anyway and says it could
not tell** (refusing on a guess is the same sin this release is about); and a
run started by a service manager **waits** instead of refusing, because launchd
and systemd treat any exit as a reason to relaunch and a refusal would become a
restart storm.

### 4. Self-update rollback now works for headless installs

The health gate that commits a self-update — or rolls back a build that cannot
start — was only ever called by the desktop app. A CLI-only rig that
auto-updated into a broken build **had no rollback at all**. The CLI now arms the
gate as its first action and confirms once the process is demonstrably up. Note
the honest limit: this proves the binary **starts**, not that it mines; a build
that crashes an hour later is not caught by it.

### 5. Payout address: checked properly, and confirmed by a human

The client used to check only the shape of your `prl1p…` return address — it
said so in its own source — and then signed an enrollment for it. A single
mistyped character was signed, sent, and rejected server-side; you found out from
a rebate that never arrived.

v0.6.8 verifies the **full bech32m checksum and the witness program** — the same
rule the server's payout boundary applies — **before anything is signed**, and
shows you the **complete, unmasked address** to confirm. It never infers consent
from silence: non-interactive use requires an explicit flag.

Behaviour changes worth knowing: `identity --set-prl-payout` refuses without
`--confirm-payout` when there is no terminal or `--json` is set; `setup
--prl-payout` needs `--yes` non-interactively; and an address already stored (or
set via env) that fails the checksum is now reported as an error everywhere
instead of being used silently.

### 6. Hostile text cannot repaint your terminal

Remote strings — model ids, roles, reason codes, HTTP error bodies — went
straight to the CLI. ANSI/OSC escapes in them can forge a success line, hide a
warning, or redraw the screen. They are now sanitised where they enter **and**
where they are printed. Model **prompts are deliberately not sanitised** (they
are program input, not display text) and tests pin that distinction.

The update button's download URL from the read API is now restricted to
`github.com` over HTTPS; anything else falls back to the built-in official
releases page.

### 7. Groundwork for encrypted mining connections

The GPU-PRL and alpha lanes ignored an endpoint declared `transport: tls` and
mined in the clear anyway. They now consume the transport field like the XMR and
RVN lanes: PRL emits `stratum+ssl://` for a TLS endpoint, and the alpha lane —
whose real engine takes a bare `host:port` with no verified TLS syntax —
**refuses** a TLS endpoint rather than silently mining in the clear.

**This changes nothing on the live network today.** Every endpoint is plaintext,
the generated command line is byte-for-byte identical to v0.6.7, and **our
relays do not terminate TLS yet** — the client half is simply ready for the day
they do.

### N. Updates that can install themselves — with the brakes built first

Until now the client updated **only** when someone typed a command or clicked a
button. That is slow, and in August 2026 slow cost a miner three days of
electricity. So v0.6.8 can install a signed update on its own — and every part of
that sentence is fenced, because automatic installation is the single most
dangerous capability a client can have. If our release key were ever stolen, an
attacker could sign a malicious build, and automatic installation is what turns
that from a problem into remote code execution on every mining machine. Rotating
the key afterwards does not un-install anything.

So the brakes came first, and they are enforced **by the client**, not by the
manifest we publish:

* **A day of daylight.** A version must have been visible to *your* machine for
  24 hours before it installs itself. The clock is local: it starts when your
  client first sees the version, not from any date in the release. A published
  release cannot shorten it — the field exists, and it can only ask for *longer*.
* **In batches.** A release names a percentage; your machine falls into a stable
  bucket, so a bad build reaches a slice of the fleet and not all of it. The
  bucket is drawn from a random local label that is never sent anywhere and is
  not derived from your hardware or your address.
* **The same version can never change its bytes.** Your client remembers the
  package hash it first saw for each version. If the same version number ever
  shows up with different bytes, it refuses, downloads nothing, and says so.
* **A kill switch that works on clients already out there.** A release can be
  **withdrawn**. A withdrawn version is never installed — not automatically, and
  not by `alice-miner update --yes` either — and a machine already running one is
  told, and reverted to its previous build where it is allowed to act.
* **It has to still work.** A build that installs itself is on probation: if it
  fails to start twice, it is rolled back and never auto-installed on that
  machine again. And if it starts but stops landing accepted shares — on a
  machine that *was* landing them before the update — it is rolled back too.
  Crucially, that second test is **switched off when the previous build was not
  earning either**, because that is what an upstream outage looks like, and
  blaming the client for the network is the exact mistake this whole release is
  about.

**The default is `security-only`:** releases we explicitly mark as security fixes
install themselves; everything else only tells you it exists. Choose differently
with `alice-miner update --auto off|notify|security-only|full`, or in Settings →
Software update. `off` means off — no check, no network, nothing.

Two things we will not pretend. First, none of the above stops a stolen release
key; it shortens the window in which one reaches everybody from "however long
until people update by hand" to about a day, and gives us a revocation switch to
use inside that day. The real fix is two-person signing, and it is not built yet.
Second, **automatic client updates would not have fixed the August 2026 outage
at all** — v0.6.5, v0.6.6 and v0.6.7 all pin the same mining engine, so a miner
on the newest client was rejected exactly as hard as one on the oldest. That
failure needs the engine pin to be updatable on its own, which is a separate
piece of work.

Mining is never interrupted for an update: a new build is swapped in on disk and
takes effect the next time you start.

### Not in this release

External Python engines for the AI and training roles still run **unsandboxed and
inherit the full environment**. This is a real design defect. Both roles are
**off by default** and server logs for 2026-07-21 → 08-04 show a single request
to those endpoints (a 404), so current exposure is zero — but it must be fixed as
its own project before the AI lane carries value, not folded into a client batch.

Encrypted mining connections are **not** delivered — only the client-side half.
`[CONFIRM-AT-RELEASE]`

## 中文

### 1. 不再编造原因的错误信息

旧文案把**任何**提到 `/m4/` 的传输层失败都渲染成"未进入允许名单"。于是公司防火墙、
做 HTTPS 中间人的路由器、杀毒软件的 TLS 扫描 —— 这些我方服务器**根本看不到**的东西 ——
统统被说成"你的地址没有注册"。三个区域同时失败让它看起来更像账户问题,而同一台机器上
的浏览器打开我们网站却毫无问题。

v0.6.8 先回答一个更窄、但**能够回答**的问题:**这个请求到底走到了哪一层?**

| 我们观测到什么 | 现在怎么说 |
|---|---|
| 收到了 HTTP 状态码 | 服务器答复了 —— 只有 `401`/`403` 才允许提"允许名单" |
| TLS / 证书失败 | 连接被拦截或证书无法验证;**不是**账户问题 |
| DNS / 连接 / 超时 | 本机联系不上服务器 |
| 收到了但读不懂 | 答复无法解析 —— 原因未知 |
| 以上都不是 | **分不清** —— 就照这四个字说 |

TLS 失败现在附带三条真正管用的自查:换手机热点重试、在安全软件里放行
`us,asia,eu.aliceprotocol.org`、用 `--region` 指定区域。`429` 明说是限流,`5xx` 明说
是**我方**故障,其它 `4xx` 明确写出"不一定是允许名单"。

同样的修正应用到了监督器的中继文案。"所有中继不可用"是一个关于**我方基础设施**的断言,
而客户端根本无从验证;现在改为**"本机联系不上任何区域中继"**。(2026-07-25 那次把
客户端看门狗 bug 误报成"区域故障"的假警报,用的正是旧措辞。)

**一条必须记录在案的更正:** 触发这项工作的审计说客户端自带根证书、不读系统证书库。
这对更早的版本成立,但 **v0.6.7 已经在所有平台读系统证书库**。所以那位矿工三区同挂的
真正原因**我们仍然不知道** —— 本机安装的拦截 CA、以及纯 TCP/DNS 封锁,都与现有证据
相符。v0.6.8 要做的,是让客户端不再假装自己知道。

### 2. 不再谎称"在线"的 AI 与训练循环

- **崩溃循环现在有界。** 一个不断把引擎搞崩的分配,过去会在下一次轮询时被当成全新分配
  而把失败预算清零 —— 同一个坏分配可以永远重启下去。现在预算跟着分配的稳定指纹跨轮询
  存活;已放弃的分配在递增冷却后只得到**一次探针**,而不是一整份新预算。真正在服务的
  时长会退还预算,从不服务的分配则不会。
- **座位丢了要去修,而不是拿话搪塞。** 座位丢失(或连续三次心跳失败)现在会**停引擎**、
  进入显式的**重新注册**状态并带退避。此前客户端会一直显示"等待调度中心分配此阶段",
  而它其实早已不在调度池里。
- **就绪判定改用正确的端口,并说明结论从哪来。** 探测用的是你本地配置的端口,而不是
  中心分配的端口。按设计不监听的 head 节点可能永远停在 `launching`,也可能因为一个
  无关程序占了端口而被判成 ready。状态行现在区分"引擎已报告正在监听"与"端口可接受连接
  (这是探测结果,并非引擎自述)"。
- **读不懂的协议答复不再归档为"等待"。** 除 `no_assignment` 外的所有状态 —— 包括服务端
  错误信封 —— 过去都渲染成一个安静正常的等待。现在任何无法识别的状态都作为协议错误
  暴露出来,并附经过清洗的状态与 reason code。
- **不完整或即将过期的租约,在 GPU 开工前就被拒。** 缺 entry point、缺 held-out 承诺、
  缺过期时间的训练租约,过去会被接受并填空串 —— 一次完整生成花在一个无法验证、或早已
  失效的任务上。现在这三个字段必填且格式校验,并在**生成前、每次重试前、提交前**三处
  检查截止时间。
- **AI/训练子进程纳入与挖矿引擎相同的进程组 / Job Object 管理**,被杀的运行不会再留下
  占着显存和带宽的后代进程。

### 3. 单实例,并且对"查不出的事"诚实

两个矿工共用一个数据目录会互相破坏账目。v0.6.8 采用原子锁:第二个实例**拒绝启动**,
并报出正在运行的 pid、共用的目录、以及可以怎么办 —— `--allow-multiple` 是写明的逃生口。

两处刻意的限制,说出来而不是藏起来:当探测**查不出**对方进程是否活着时,矿工**照常启动
并明说自己查不出**(靠猜来拒绝,正是本版本要清除的那种毛病);由服务管理器拉起的运行则
**等待**而不是拒绝,因为 launchd 和 systemd 把任何退出都当成重新拉起的理由,拒绝会变成
重启风暴。

### 4. 无界面安装现在也能回滚自更新

那道"确认自更新 / 回滚起不来的构建"的健康门,一直以来只有桌面应用会调用。纯 CLI 的矿机
自动更新到一个坏构建后**完全没有回滚**。现在 CLI 在最早的位置武装这道门,并在进程确实
起来后确认。诚实的边界:它证明的是这个二进制**能启动**,不是它能挖矿;一小时后才崩的
构建,它抓不到。

### 5. 返还地址:真校验,并且由人确认

客户端过去只检查 `prl1p…` 返还地址的**形状** —— 源码里自己写明了这一点 —— 然后就为它
签名注册。打错一个字符照签照发,由服务端拒绝;而你是从"返还一直没到"才知道的。

v0.6.8 在**签名之前**验证**完整的 bech32m 校验和与 witness 程序** —— 与服务端付款边界
所用的是同一条规则 —— 并把**完整、不打码的地址**显示给你确认。它绝不从沉默推断同意:
非交互使用必须带显式 flag。

需要知道的行为变化:无终端或带 `--json` 时,`identity --set-prl-payout` 不带
`--confirm-payout` 会被拒绝;`setup --prl-payout` 非交互需要 `--yes`;已经存好(或从环境
变量读入)但校验和错误的地址,现在处处报错,而不是被静默使用。

### 6. 恶意文本无法重绘你的终端

远端字符串 —— model id、role、reason code、HTTP 错误正文 —— 过去直接进 CLI 输出。其中的
ANSI/OSC 转义可以伪造一行成功、隐藏一条警告、或者整屏重绘。现在它们在**进入时**和
**打印时**各清洗一次。模型 **prompt 刻意不清洗**(它是程序输入,不是显示文本),并有测试
钉死这条区分。

更新按钮从 read API 拿到的下载地址,现在被限制为 HTTPS 的 `github.com`;其它一律回退到
内置的官方 releases 页。

### 7. 为加密挖矿连接铺路

GPU-PRL 与 alpha lane 过去会忽略端点上声明的 `transport: tls`,照样明文挖。现在它们像
XMR / RVN lane 一样真正消费 transport 字段:PRL 对 TLS 端点输出 `stratum+ssl://`;
而 alpha lane —— 其真实引擎接受的是裸 `host:port`、我们**从未验证过它的 TLS 语法** ——
对 TLS 端点选择**拒绝**,而不是嘴上 tls、手上明文。

**这在今天的线上网络零变化。** 所有端点都是明文,生成的命令行与 v0.6.7 逐字节相同,
而且**我方中继目前还没有 TLS 端**可连 —— 客户端这一半只是先就位。

### N. 可以自己安装的更新 —— 但刹车先装好

在此之前,客户端**只有**在有人敲命令或点按钮时才更新。这很慢,而 2026 年 8 月,慢让
一位矿工白烧了三天电。所以 v0.6.8 可以自行安装已签名的更新 —— 而这句话的每一部分都
被围了栏,因为**自动安装是一个客户端能拥有的最危险的能力**。如果我们的发布密钥被盗,
攻击者就能签出恶意版本,而自动安装会把这件事从"一个问题"变成"每一台矿机上的远程
代码执行"。事后轮换密钥,并不能把已经装上的东西卸下来。

所以刹车先装,而且由**客户端**强制执行,不由我们发布的清单决定:

* **晾一天。** 一个版本必须在**你这台机器**上被看见满 24 小时,才可能自行安装。这个
  时钟是本地的:从你的客户端第一次看到该版本开始算,而不是从发布日期算。发布清单
  **无法缩短它** —— 那个字段存在,但只能要求**更长**。
* **分批放量。** 一次发布会写明百分比;你的机器落在一个稳定的分组里,于是坏版本只会
  触达舰队的一部分,而不是全部。分组来自一个**随机的本地标签**,它从不上传,也不由
  你的硬件或地址推导而来。
* **同一个版本号,字节永远不能变。** 客户端会记住每个版本首次见到的安装包哈希。如果
  同一个版本号后来带着不同的字节出现,它会拒绝、不下载任何东西,并把这件事说出来。
* **对已经装出去的客户端也生效的紧急开关。** 一次发布可以被**撤回**。被撤回的版本永远
  不会被安装 —— 自动不会,`alice-miner update --yes` 也不会 —— 而已经在运行该版本的
  机器会被告知,并在其设置允许自动动作时回退到上一个版本。
* **装上之后还得能用。** 自行安装的版本处于**观察期**:两次启动失败就回滚,并且此后
  再也不会在这台机器上自动安装。若它能启动、却不再有被接受的份额 —— 而这台机器在更新
  **之前是有的** —— 同样回滚。关键在于:当**上一个版本也同样没有收益**时,第二项检测
  **自动关闭**,因为那正是上游故障的样子;把网络的问题算到客户端头上,恰恰是本次发布
  从头到尾想要纠正的那个错误。

**默认值是 `security-only`:** 我们明确标记为安全修复的版本会自行安装,其余版本只提示。
用 `alice-miner update --auto off|notify|security-only|full` 修改,或在"设置 → 软件更新"
里选。`off` 就是彻底关闭 —— 不检查、不联网、什么都不做。

有两件事我们不会假装。第一,上面这些**挡不住被盗的发布密钥**;它只是把"被盗密钥触达
所有人"的窗口,从"等大家自己手动更新的那么久"压缩到大约一天,并让我们在这一天里有一个
撤回开关可用。真正的根治是**双人签名**,它还没有做。第二,**自动客户端更新根本解决不了
2026 年 8 月那次事故** —— v0.6.5、v0.6.6、v0.6.7 pin 的是同一个挖矿引擎,用最新客户端的
矿工被拒得和用最旧客户端的一模一样。那次故障需要的是"引擎 pin 能独立更新",那是另一件事。

更新永远不会打断挖矿:新版本在磁盘上就位,下次启动时生效。

### 本版不做

AI 与训练角色调用的外部 Python 引擎,仍然**没有沙箱、继承全部环境变量**。这是真实的设计
缺陷。两个角色**默认关闭**,且 2026-07-21 → 08-04 的服务端日志显示这两个端点总共只有
1 条请求(还是 404),因此**当前暴露为零** —— 但它必须作为独立工程修好,在 AI lane 承载
价值之前,而不是塞进一个客户端批次里。

加密挖矿连接**并未交付**,只交付了客户端这一半。`[CONFIRM-AT-RELEASE]`
